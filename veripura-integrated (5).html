VeriLiquid · Act 2

The billion-dollar layer.

The $10 trillion global trade finance gap exists because risk cannot be assessed on unverified cargo. VeriLiquid solves this with a regulated real-world asset (RWA) tokenisation structure — a DeFi-adjacent model in which attested trade receivables are tokenised, held in a regulated SPV, and made available to crypto-native and private-credit investors. VeriPura's attestation data is what makes the risk model defensible.

Mechanic 01

RWA Tokenisation via Regulated SPV

A trade finance SPV acquires LC-backed or invoice-backed receivables from the importer. The cash-flow stream is tokenised on IOTA as a real-world asset (RWA) — structurally equivalent to an asset-backed security, but represented on-chain and settled via smart contract. The SPV sits in a regulated jurisdiction (Luxembourg or UAE). KYC/AML and whitelisted investor access apply. No attestation from VeriPura, no token. No token, no financing.

Mechanic 02

DeFi-Adjacent Yield & Collateral Pools

Token holders interact via RWA-enabled DeFi protocols — not open permissionless pools, but regulated structures with whitelisted assets and KYC/AML gates. They can deposit tokens into yield pools pegged to the underlying trade returns, or use them as collateral for stablecoin loans. Payment to the exporter/supplier triggers automatically via smart contract on attestation confirmation. The importer's cash cycle shortens. The supplier's risk drops. The investor earns a yield anchored to a verified physical trade flow.

How a VeriLiquid transaction works
01
Shipment attested by VeriPura

Compliance documentation verified and written immutably to the IOTA blockchain before the shipment leaves origin. This attestation record is the risk foundation of the entire flow.

02
SPV acquires the receivable

A regulated trade finance SPV (Luxembourg or UAE) purchases the LC-backed or invoice-backed receivable from the importer. The underlying asset is a classic trade finance instrument — the SPV provides the regulated wrapper.

03
Receivable tokenised as an RWA on IOTA

The SPV's cash-flow stream is represented on-chain as a real-world asset token. The token carries the VeriPura attestation record as verifiable metadata — linking the financial instrument to the proven compliance event. Whitelisted investors and KYC/AML checks enforced at protocol level.

04
Investors deploy capital via DeFi-adjacent pools

Token holders deposit into regulated RWA yield pools (yield pegged to the underlying trade) or use tokens as collateral for stablecoin loans. Capital sources include crypto-native funds, private credit and institutional investors — all interacting via tokens, with the underlying asset remaining a classic trade receivable.

05
Smart contract settles on confirmed delivery

On final attestation and delivery confirmation, the smart contract releases payment to the exporter automatically. The SPV is settled, the token redeemed, and the importer's working capital freed for the next shipment.
